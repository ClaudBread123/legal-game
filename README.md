# Legal Game

An interactive legal simulation game for learning and practicing legal concepts.

## Overview

Legal Game puts players in the role of a legal professional navigating real-world scenarios — from contract disputes to courtroom strategy. Learn the law by doing.

## Features

- Case simulations with branching decisions
- Score tracking based on legal accuracy
- Multiple practice areas (contracts, torts, criminal law)
